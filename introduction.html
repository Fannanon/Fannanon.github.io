Introduction

My name is Damon and I like to ride my bicycle. On a weekday, I work for the Western Australian government at the Department of Transport.

A series of key life events led to my decision to head off and attempt this grand adventure. The first came on 25 December 2013 when my lovely girlfriend Dee presented me with my first road bike as a completely unexpected Christmas present. From there, I developed something of an obsession with riding and bikes in general. The mechanical simplicity of the bicycle and its ability to propel me across the city on a bowl of cereal and a banana definitely appealed to something within me.

Skipping forward a few years, Dee and I went through a year apart while she taught English in Japan. We were able to keep our relationship strong despite the distance, mostly thanks to modern technology and a couple of very nice holidays together over the course of the year. If you asked her how Japan was, Dee would probably tell you that her time there was an amazing and life altering experience. Sitting at home in Perth following one of our nightly phone conversations, I wondered what my equivalent life experience might be. It’s true, I was one of the first paladins on my World of Warcraft server to complete the epic mount quest back in vanilla, but I wondered if this was something I could tell my kids about one day? Maybe, but surely I could do a bit better…

The seed was planted in that moment, but it took the combination of a few more things to push me into crazy guy on a bike territory. The lease on my home of nearly 5 years was up for renewal and a role that I was far too attached to at work was coming to an end.

So here I am, two weeks out from my departure date writing the introduction to what I hope will be a long and semi-interesting account of my time touring the United States on a bicycle.

I’m hoping to raise a bit of money for charity while I ride. I’ve chosen to sponsor Beyond Blue, a fantastic organisation who work in the Australian community to raise awareness of depression and anxiety. A link to donate is below. This journal is more a collection of thoughts and isn’t intended as a promotional tool for fundraising, but if you do happen to read this journal and find it useful or entertaining, consider dropping a dime in the collection hat!
